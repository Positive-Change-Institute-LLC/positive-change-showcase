# Arcana Enterprise NFT Product Proposal

## Status

The supplied `arcana_nfts` list is a static proposal, not checked-in product data or an NFT implementation. Its three records include proposed prices and subscription tiers, `auto_evolution` flags, utility lists, QR labels, wallet URIs, and checkout URLs. None establishes that an NFT is issued, a service is available, a payment can be made, or the listed prices are approved.

The checkout URLs, `xrpl://` values, and strings such as `QR_FORGE_001_ABC123` are unverified data; the QR strings are labels, not generated QR images. No wallet, XRPL, payment, cross-chain, governance, or evolution service is connected.

## Keep identifiers and claims separate

The checked-in Arcana showcase has static concept IDs `FORGE001`, `RELICS001`, and `ASCEND001`. The proposal uses `FORGE-001`, `RELICS-001`, and `ASCEND-001`; do not treat these as equivalent product IDs without an approved crosswalk and tests.

The proposed prices, rarity, “AI-powered” behavior, utility, trademarks, and ® symbols are not verified commercial or legal facts. Preserve them as proposed metadata only, and do not copy them into the Shopify/Whop catalogs without approval and evidence.

## Interoperability

There is no shared catalog, minting contract, payment endpoint, QR generator, or wallet connection for these records. A future integration needs a reviewed source of truth, validated IDs and pricing, verified destinations, explicit user authorization, and tested read/write boundaries.
